Amit Ashkenazi's paper proposes new methods for predicting certainty bounds in highly nonlinear parameter estimation problems.

The work addresses a fundamental challenge in biomechanical modeling: quantifying the uncertainty of material parameters identified from experimental data when the underlying model is strongly nonlinear. The proposed framework provides reliable confidence bounds that are critical for safe patient-specific device design.
