Check out Dror Raf's [new paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mechmat.2025.105534) in *Mechanics of Materials* on Soft orthotropic hyperelastic lattice structures. In this work, we develop a method for numerically homogenizing the hyperelastic anisotropy of thick-beamed lattice structures under large tension, compression, and shear deformations. The homogenization helps reduce the simulation time by 2-3 orders of magnitude while preserving accurate predictions of force and full-field deformations. The results have been experimentally validated on lattice structures fabricated from TPU using SLS.
