We are thrilled to announce that our lab has been awarded two prestigious grants from the Israel Science Foundation (ISF)!

The grants will support our research into in-vivo soft tissue biomechanics and the development of patient-specific prosthetic and orthotic devices. We are grateful to the ISF for this recognition and look forward to the exciting research ahead.
