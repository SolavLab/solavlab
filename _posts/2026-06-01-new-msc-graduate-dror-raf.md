Congratulations to Dror Raf, who successfully defended his thesis and graduated with an MSc in Mechanical Engineering!

Dror's research focused on soft orthotropic hyperelastic lattice structures, developing a numerical homogenization method that reduces simulation time by 2–3 orders of magnitude while preserving accurate mechanical predictions under large deformations.

We wish Dror all the best in his next steps!
