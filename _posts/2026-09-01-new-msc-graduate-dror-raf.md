Congratulations to Dror Raf, who defended his thesis and graduated with an MSc in Mechanical Engineering!

Dror's research focused on soft orthotropic hyperelastic lattice structures. His paper in Mechanics of Materials develops a method for numerically homogenizing the hyperelastic anisotropy of thick-beamed lattice structures under large tension, compression, and shear deformations — reducing simulation time by 2–3 orders of magnitude while preserving accurate predictions.

We wish Dror all the best in his next steps!
