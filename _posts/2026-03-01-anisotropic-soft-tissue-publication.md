Amit Ashkenazi's new paper in the *Journal of the Mechanics and Physics of Solids* investigates indentation-based identification of anisotropic constitutive parameters of soft tissues.

The study proposes and validates a method for characterizing the anisotropic mechanical properties of soft biological tissues using indentation experiments combined with inverse finite-element analysis — a minimally invasive approach with direct clinical relevance.
