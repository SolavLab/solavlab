> We are currently recruiting postdocs and graduate students!

**Graduate students:**

Check out the [Technion graduate school page](https://graduate.technion.ac.il/) and the [Mechanical Engineering programs](https://meeng.technion.ac.il/graduate-programs/) and acceptance criteria.

## International postdocs:

Check out the following funding programs that can help you land in our lab. If interested, please contact us so we can work on your application together!

- **Fulbright**: for U.S. citizens
  [https://www.fulbright.org.il/postdoctoral](https://www.fulbright.org.il/postdoctoral)
- **Azrieli**: for EU, EFTA, UK or Canada citizens
  [https://azrielifoundation.org/fellows/internationalpostdoctoral/](https://azrielifoundation.org/fellows/internationalpostdoctoral/)
- **Zuckerman**: for U.S./Canada citizens
  [https://zuckermanstem.org/ourprograms/postdoc-program/](https://zuckermanstem.org/ourprograms/postdoc-program/)
- **EuroTech** (must be a collaboration with one of these 4 universities: Technical University of Denmark, L’École Polytechnique à Paris, Eindhoven University of Technology, Technical University of Munich)
  [https://postdoc2.eurotech-universities.eu/information/applicants/](https://postdoc2.eurotech-universities.eu/information/applicants/)

For all inquiries, please send an email to {% include email.html address="danas@technion.ac.il" %} with your CV and transcript.
