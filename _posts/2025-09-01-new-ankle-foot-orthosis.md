We have developed a new ankle-foot orthosis (AFO) that allows natural walking with complete or partial offloading of the ankle and foot.

The device is designed for patients recovering from foot and ankle injuries or surgeries, enabling them to remain ambulatory during healing. Unlike conventional offloading solutions, this design preserves a natural gait pattern while effectively reducing mechanical load on the affected region.
