Dror Raf's paper in *Mechanics of Materials* develops a numerical homogenization method for predicting the hyperelastic anisotropy of thick-beamed lattice structures under large tension, compression, and shear deformations.

The method reduces simulation time by 2–3 orders of magnitude compared to full-scale finite element analysis while maintaining high accuracy — enabling practical design optimization of patient-specific soft orthotic devices with tailored mechanical properties.
