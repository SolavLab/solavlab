SoBIG

Research

Our research bridges biomechanics, medical imaging, computational modeling, and device fabrication. We develop tools and methods that bring us closer to patient-specific prosthetics and orthotics that truly fit — mechanically and biologically.

01

Parameter Identification

Characterizing the mechanical properties of soft biological tissues using indentation experiments and inverse finite-element methods.

Learn more →

02

Ankle-Foot Offloading Gait

Analyzing and optimizing gait biomechanics for patients who require partial or complete offloading of the ankle and foot.

Learn more →

03

Novel Ankle-Foot Orthosis

Designing a new AFO that allows natural walking with complete or partial offloading, improving patient comfort and mobility.

Learn more →

04

Hyperelastic Lattice Structures

Developing numerical homogenization methods for soft orthotropic hyperelastic lattice structures used in patient-specific devices.

Learn more →

05

DuoDIC

A MATLAB toolbox for 3D full-field surface deformation measurement using two-camera stereo digital image correlation.

Learn more →

06

MultiDIC

An open-source MATLAB toolbox for multi-camera 3D digital image correlation, enabling full 360° surface strain measurement.

Learn more →

07

Breast FEA
