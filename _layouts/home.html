The Lab

Towards enhanced comfort and health for prosthetics and orthotics users

Our group studies the biomechanical interface between the human body and biomedical devices such as prosthetics and orthotics. Our main goal is to develop and optimize patient-specific devices in order to improve patient comfort, health, and function.

To achieve this, we analyze the biomechanical factors that affect human movement and function, and develop new imaging and measurement tools, computational algorithms, experimental procedures, and fabrication methods.

Explore our research

Research areas

  • Musculoskeletal biomechanics
  • Prosthetics & orthotics
  • Parameter identification
  • Digital Image Correlation (DuoDIC / MultiDIC)
  • Hyperelastic lattice structures
  • Ankle-foot offloading
  • Novel orthosis design
  • Breast finite-element analysis
