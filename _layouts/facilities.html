SoBIG

Facilities

Our lab is located in the Dan Kahn Building (room 308) at Technion's Faculty of Mechanical Engineering. We are equipped with state-of-the-art experimental and computational tools for biomechanical research.

Motion Capture System

High-speed optical motion capture system for measuring 3D human movement kinematics during walking, running, and other functional activities.

3D Digital Image Correlation

Multi-camera stereo DIC setup for full-field 3D surface deformation and strain measurement of soft tissues and devices under loading.

Mechanical Testing

Universal testing machines and custom fixtures for mechanical characterization of soft biological tissues and flexible materials under various loading conditions.

Indentation System

Custom-built robotic indentation system for in-vivo mechanical characterization of soft tissues, combined with imaging for multi-modal measurement.

3D Printing & Fabrication

Additive manufacturing equipment for rapid prototyping of patient-specific orthotic and prosthetic devices, including flexible and rigid materials.

Computational Cluster

High-performance computing resources for finite element simulations, parameter optimization, and large-scale biomechanical modeling.
